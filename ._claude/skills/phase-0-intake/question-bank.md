# Intake question bank (business-analyst picks only what is still unknown)

## Goal & success
- What should change for users' lives after using the app? What's the one outcome that matters most?
- What does success look like in 3/6/12 months (downloads, active users, revenue, ratings)?
- Is this a side project, a portfolio of small apps, or a long-term product?

## Users & problem
- Who exactly is the primary user (age, level, context, country, language)? Secondary users?
- What do they use today instead? What frustrates them about it?
- When and where will they use the app (commute, at desk, daily habit, occasional)? Session length?

## Scope
- The 3 things the app absolutely must do in the first version?
- What should the app explicitly NOT do?
- Accounts needed? Sync across devices? Offline use? Social/sharing? User-generated content?
- Content source: who creates the content (us, AI, users, licensed)? Who maintains it?
- Web presence: a landing/marketing site? A web version of the product (in the browser, installable PWA)? A customer account page (subscription, data export, account deletion)? Dashboards for partners or creators?
- Running the app after launch: who manages content, users, support requests, refunds, reported content, push campaigns? Is a BaaS console enough, or do you need an admin website (how many staff, which roles)?

## Platforms & markets
- iOS, Android, or both at launch? Tablets? Apple Watch / widgets?
- Countries and languages at launch? Later?

## Monetization
- Free, freemium, subscription, one-time purchase, ads? Any model you refuse?
- Price sensitivity of the audience; acceptable time before revenue?

## Constraints
- Budget for paid services (BaaS paid tier, AI API costs, fonts, illustrations, sounds)? Monthly ceiling?
- Deadline or target launch window?
- Regulated domain (health, finance, kids under 13, education for minors)?
- Apple/Google developer accounts: already created (personal)? Can you recruit ≥12 Android testers for 14 days?

## Brand & experience
- 3 adjectives for how the app should feel. Apps whose look/feel you love or hate, and why?
- Mascot/illustrations/sound — welcome or not? Dark mode required?

## AI
- Should the app use AI? For what (tutor, feedback, generation, personalization)?
- Limits: cost per user per month, privacy (no user data to third-party AI?), must work offline?

## Hard parts (dig in)
- What happens when the user is wrong / fails / stops using it for a week?
- Moderation and abuse: can users harm each other or misuse AI?
- Trust: why would users believe the app's feedback/content is correct?
