# Takeover question bank (business-analyst picks only what is still unknown)

The PO often cannot say exactly what is wrong. Ask about moments, comparisons and decisions, not about diagnosis.

## Goal and money
- What should this app achieve in 6 and 12 months: first paying users, a revenue number, a portfolio piece, a personal tool?
- Who do you imagine paying, how much, and for what exactly?
- What is the budget for running it (servers, AI calls, content, design assets) and for this engagement (Claude plan or API)?

## Symptoms (the moments)
- When you open the app, at which moment do you feel "this is not it yet"? Show us or name the screen.
- Which app do you compare it with when it disappoints you? What does that app do better?
- If a friend tried it today, where would they give up?
- What did you build that you now think nobody will use?
- What took the most time or money so far, and was it worth it?

## Users and use
- Who is the user you are sure about? Who is the user you are hoping for?
- Has anyone besides you used it? What did they say or do?
- Is there any usage data, crash data, store review or feedback?

## Attachments and freedom
- What must stay (brand, a feature, a method, content, data, the tech stack)? Why?
- What may we cut or replace, even if it was expensive?
- Are there users or data today that a rebuild must not lose?

## Way of working
- Which branch holds the latest work? Is anything unpushed or unmerged that we should include?
- Will you keep changing the app yourself during the engagement? (We recommend not to: we work on `company/*` branches and you merge our pull requests.)
- Which of the repo's own rules matter to you, and which came from the old workflow?

## Release
- Is the app published anywhere (App Store, Google Play, TestFlight, internal)? Under which account and bundle ID?
- Do you have Apple and Google developer accounts? Can you recruit 12 Android testers for 14 days?
- Is there a deadline or a launch window?
